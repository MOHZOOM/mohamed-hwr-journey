Hi, I’m Mohamed

From Libya to Berlin.

Hi, I’m Mohamed, a Libyan graduate with a background in Business Information Technology. I’m from Libya. In 2022, I moved to Jordan to study Business Information Technology at PSUT. A few years later, I spent a semester in Varna, Bulgaria, through Erasmus+. Now I’m in Berlin, starting my master’s at HWR. The places are different, and so was everyday life in each one. Alongside studying, I tried out business development, software development, banking, and digital-rights work. I’m still learning where these interests will take me, but I know I want to understand how organisations use technology and data.

What connects it all

Why I chose this master’s

Whether I was looking at a business challenge, working with software, or creating digital-awareness content, I kept coming back to the same question: how can we turn information into something useful?

That question is a big reason I chose to continue into Business Intelligence and Process Management. I want to improve my ability to analyse data, understand processes, and help organisations make better-informed decisions.
